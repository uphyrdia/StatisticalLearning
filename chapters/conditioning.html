Let \((\Omega,\mathscr{H},\mathbb{P})\) be a probability space.

Conditional expectation

Let \(\mathscr{E}\subset\mathscr{H}\) be a sub-\(\sigma\)-algebra of \(\mathscr{H}\).

The conditional expectation of a positive random variable \(X\in\overline{\mathscr{H}}_+\) given \(\mathscr{E}\) is any positive random variable \(U\in \overline{\mathscr{E}}_+\) such that

\[ \mathbb{E}(V X)=\mathbb{E}(V U)\qquad \forall V\in \overline{\mathscr{E}}_+ \]

\(U\in \overline{\mathscr{E}}_+\) is the conditional expectation of \(X\in\overline{\mathscr{H}}_+\) given \(\mathscr{E}\) if and only if

\[ \mathbb{E}(\mathbbm{1}_AX)=\mathbb{E}(\mathbbm{1}_AU)\qquad \forall A\in\mathscr{E} \]
Proof

Suppose \(x_n\to x\). By , choose \(N\geq1\) such that \(|x_n-x|\lt1\) whenever \(n\geq N\).

For those indices, the triangle inequality gives

\[ |x_n|\leq |x_n-x|+|x|\lt 1+|x|. \]

Thus the entire sequence is bounded by

\[ M=\max\bigl(\{|x_k|:1\leq k\leq N\}\cup\{1+|x|\}\bigr). \]

□

Uniqueness

If both \(X'\) and \(X''\) are conditional expectations of \(X\in\overline{\mathscr{H}}_+\) given \(\mathscr{E}\), then \(X'=X''\) almost surely.
